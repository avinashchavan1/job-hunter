New jobs every morning, ranked against your resume.

Job Hunter checks job boards, company career pages and your job-alert emails, scores each posting against your resume, and tells you plainly what fits and what doesn't. You decide what to apply for.

Open Job Hunter Invite only — ask the admin to add your email.

How it works

  1. Add a resume. It reads your titles, skills and experience once and suggests what to search for.
  2. It checks every morning. New postings from every source are collected and duplicates merged.
  3. You review a short list. Each job has a match score, the reasons, the gaps, and a cover note ready to paste.

What you get

Match score with reasons

A 0–100 score for every job, why it fits, what to watch out for, and which of the posting's skills you have or lack.

Flags, not filters

City, experience, salary and words you want to avoid show as green, amber or red. Nothing is hidden — Secunderabad counts as Hyderabad, Chennai shows as “South India”.

More places searched

Job search APIs covering LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed and company sites, remote boards, the career pages of companies you watch, and LinkedIn and Naukri alert emails from your Gmail.

Repost and scam check

Copies on aggregator sites are marked, with a link to find the original on the company's own careers page.

Cover notes

A short note for each job, written from your real experience — no invented facts, no placeholders.

Application tracker

Shortlist, applied, interview, offer: one board for every application, with notes.

Questions

Does it apply for me?
No. It finds, ranks and explains jobs. You open the posting and apply yourself.
What does it read in my Gmail?
Only job-alert emails from job sites (LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed and similar), with read-only access you can remove at any time.
Who can see my data?
Only people the admin has invited. Keys for job sites and the AI stay on the server and never reach the browser.
Who can use it?
It's invite-only. Once your email is added, sign in with Google or with an email and password.
